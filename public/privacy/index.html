Privacy policy

Multistream · Last updated 9 November 2026

Multistream is a desktop app that sends a church service live to YouTube, Facebook and other sites at the same time. This page explains what it does with your information. In short: everything stays on the computer the app runs on. We do not run a server that collects your data, and we do not sell or share it.

Signing in with Google (YouTube)

When you press Connect YouTube, Google asks you to let Multistream manage your YouTube account. The app uses that permission only to:

  • create the live stream (broadcast) on your channel, with the title, description, privacy setting and thumbnail you choose;
  • start and end that live stream, and change its title while you are live;
  • read the live chat so you can see it in the app;
  • read the number of people watching, likes and views of your live stream;
  • upload a recording of your service to your channel, only if you turn that on.

It does not read your email, contacts, other videos' data beyond your own live stream, or anything else in your Google account.

Signing in with Facebook

When you sign in with Facebook, the app uses your Page's permission only to create the live video on the Page you pick, change its title, and read its comments, viewer count and reactions.

Where your information is kept

  • Your sign-ins (Google and Facebook tokens) and stream keys are saved only on your computer, encrypted (Windows: locked to your Windows user; Mac: the Keychain).
  • Your scenes, settings and recordings are saved only on your computer.
  • Live chat messages are shown in the app while you are live and are not saved or sent anywhere else.
  • The app talks directly to YouTube, Facebook and the other sites you stream to. Nothing passes through a Multistream server.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Multistream's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • In your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Multistream, Remove access.
  • In Facebook: Settings, Business integrations, remove Multistream.
  • Deleting the app's settings folder (in the app's menu: Show Settings Folder) removes every sign-in and setting it saved.
