    Who can watch
    Each collection is a set of scenes. A tall (9:16) one is for Instagram or TikTok.

    Start here

    1. Add your channels. On Go Live, press Set up for YouTube or Facebook, or + Add for any other site (you need its server address and stream key).
    2. Make your scenes. Open the Studio. A scene is one screen, like Welcome or Camera. Press + under Sources to add a camera, screen, picture, name bar and more.
    3. Add sound. In the Studio's Audio Mixer, press Add and pick your microphone or sound desk. Watch the meter move when you talk.
    4. Press Start Streaming in the Studio. The app now has your video, but nobody sees it yet.
    5. Go live. On Go Live, type a title and press Go live on each channel (or let the channels with a stream key start by themselves).

    Fix a problem

    The meters move but nobody hears me
    Check the input is heard in the scene (the eye next to its name in the mixer) and not muted. Check the channel is live on Go Live.
    Echo or feedback
    Turn off Listen in headphones, or use headphones. If two inputs pick up the same sound, mute one.
    Camera not found
    Close other apps that use the camera (Zoom, Teams, the Camera app), unplug it and plug it in again, then pick it again in its settings.
    A channel keeps dropping
    Open Stream health. If Sending goes up and down, lower the quality in Studio Settings, Output, or use a cable instead of Wi-Fi.
    Screen or window is black on a Mac
    System Settings, Privacy and Security, Screen Recording: turn on Multistream, then open the app again.
    Facebook says the stream is too big
    Facebook takes 1080p at most. The app sends Facebook a smaller copy by itself; keep the cap on in the channel's settings.
    Wrong video played at the start
    Open Auto start on Go Live and check the steps, or keep Ask me first each time turned on.
