Stream everywhere at once

Multistream is a free desktop app for Windows and Mac. Build your picture in Production (cameras, screens, slides, name bars, countdowns) and go live on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and more at the same time, from one computer. Made for churches, events and teams.

What it does

Go live on every channel

One button starts the stream on YouTube, Facebook and any site that takes a stream key. Each channel runs on its own, so one dropping does not stop the others.

Production

Scenes and sources like OBS: cameras, capture cards, screens, windows, pictures, videos, text with a background, name bars and countdowns, with transitions.

Sound mixer

Bring in your sound desk, set levels in percent, listen in headphones, and a built-in limiter keeps the stream from crackling.

Multiview

See every scene at once on a second screen and switch with one click.

Recordings

Record on the computer and, if you want, upload to YouTube when the stream ends. Finished files can go to a Done folder.

Virtual camera

Send the Production picture into Zoom or Teams as a webcam (Windows).

Install help

Multistream is free and not yet signed by Microsoft or Apple, so the first time you open it your computer asks if you are sure. This is normal.

Windows

  1. Open Multistream-Setup.exe.
  2. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box shows, press More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Multistream opens by itself when it is installed.

Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Multistream into Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click Multistream in Applications and choose Open, then Open again.
  3. After that it opens normally.

How Multistream uses your YouTube account

When you connect YouTube, Multistream asks Google for permission to manage your YouTube account. It uses that permission only to:

  • create the live stream on your channel with the title, description, privacy and thumbnail you choose;
  • start and end the live stream, and change its title while you are live;
  • show the live chat, viewers and likes in the app;
  • upload the recording of your service to your channel, only if you turn that on.

Your sign-in is kept encrypted on your own computer. It is never sent to a Multistream server and never shared. Read the full privacy policy.
