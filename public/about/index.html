Multistream

Stream your service everywhere at once

Multistream is a free desktop app for Windows and Mac, made for churches. Make the picture in the Studio (cameras, slides, name bars, countdowns) and go live on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and more at the same time, from one computer.

How Multistream uses your YouTube account

When you connect YouTube, Multistream asks Google for permission to manage your YouTube account. It uses that permission only to:

  • create the live stream on your channel with the title, description, privacy and thumbnail you choose;
  • start and end the live stream, and change its title while you are live;
  • show the live chat, viewers and likes in the app;
  • upload the recording of your service to your channel, only if you turn that on.

Your sign-in is kept encrypted on your own computer. It is never sent to a Multistream server and never shared. Read the full privacy policy.
